Ultima II textured dungeon pictures
===================================

With the Standard, Classic (v1.0) and Alternate (Wiltshire) EGA graphics modes and the "Use textured
dungeon walls" option, the first person view in the dungeons and towers is drawn from the pictures in
files/ultima2/dungeons, and the monsters in it from the pictures in files/ultima2/monsters, rather
than from lines and blocks. Any picture that is missing is drawn the old way, so they can be replaced
one at a time. (The Commodore 64 mode keeps its own look.)

The colors
----------
The EGA modes use 16 colors, in palette entries 16 to 31, and the rest of the 256 are free. A standard
set of colors fills entries 48 to 255 (ramps of grays, stone, wood, rust, moss, gold, iron, flesh, red,
green, blue, pale yellow, purple and cyan, each from dark to light). They are defined in the RAMPS in
gen_ultima2_dungeons.py, which writes them to files/ultima2/dungeons/palette.pal (208 colors, 8 bits
each of red, green and blue) for the game to load. Every PNG has the whole 256 color palette in it, so
they look right in an editor, but the game draws the palette indexes. Draw with entries 48 to 255 so
that the pictures look the same in every EGA mode. Entry 0 is transparent (the PNGs have a tRNS chunk
saying so).

The dungeon pictures
--------------------
256x128 pixel PNGs, which are layers the size of the corridor (it's drawn 16 pixels in from the left
and top of the game screen). Their shapes are worked out from the same tables as the original wire
frame view (views/dungeon_map.cpp). Depth 0 is the square the party is on, and each is drawn at the
depths it can be seen at:

  front_0..7              the wall at the end of the corridor
  left_0..6, right_0..6   the walls at the sides
  leftopen_0..6, rightopen_0..6   openings in the sides, which show the far wall of the passage
  frontdoor_0..7          a door in the wall at the end
  leftdoor_0..6, rightdoor_0..6   doors in the walls at the sides
  ladderup_0..6, ladderdown_0..6  a ladder up through the ceiling, and one down through the floor
  chest_0..6              a chest
  doorway                 the wall seen from a doorway the party is standing in, with the way through
                          left open

Ladders and chests are drawn last, farthest first, so that they're over any walls drawn after them.

The monsters
------------
monsters/monster_K_D.png, for the 7 kinds of monster K in the order of the game's dungeon monsters
(1 imp, 2 ghost, 3 jellyfish, 4 serpent, 5 gremlin, 6 horned demon, 7 winged demon) at each depth D from
1 to 7 squares down the corridor. Each is a 256x128 layer in the same geometry. The monster stands in the
middle with its feet on the floor of that depth, and is about as tall as the corridor is there (57 pixels
at depth 1, half that at depth 2, and so on).

Regenerating the stand-ins
--------------------------
python gen_ultima2_dungeons.py ../../../engines/ultima/ultima2/views/dungeon_map.cpp ../files/ultima2/dungeons
python gen_ultima2_monsters.py ../../../engines/ultima/ultima2/views/dungeon_map.cpp ../files/ultima2/monsters

Adding a .png name after those arguments also draws a preview of some views (dungeons) or of the
monsters at the first depths. The stand-ins are made by programs, written with the help of an AI
assistant (Claude), and are meant to be replaced by hand drawn art.
