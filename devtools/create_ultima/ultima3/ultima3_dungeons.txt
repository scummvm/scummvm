Ultima III textured dungeon pictures
====================================

With the VGA graphics mode (the default of the enhanced game) and the "Use textured dungeon walls"
option, the first person view in dungeons is drawn from the pictures in files/ultima3/dungeons
rather than from lines. Any picture that is missing is drawn the old way, so they can be replaced
one at a time.

Format
------
* 128x128 pixel PNG, paletted with the 256 colors of the VGA mode (files/ultima3/u3vga.pal). The
  game draws the palette indexes, so it doesn't matter what palette is in the PNG, but keep to the
  one in the file so that they look right in an editor. Entries that are all the same cyan-green
  (2dffd2) aren't used for anything and shouldn't be used.
* Palette entry 0 is transparent (the PNG has a tRNS chunk saying so). Black can be drawn with a
  near black entry such as 145 (040404).
* Every picture is a layer the size of the view, so a piece goes where it sits in the view. The
  view is the area 32 pixels in from the left and top of the game screen.

The pieces
----------
The places in view are numbered as in gfx/dungeon_view.cpp. 0 is the square the party is on, 1 and 2
are the squares to its left and right, 3, 4 and 5 are the squares in the row ahead (left, middle,
right), and so on, going outwards and away. The shapes of the pieces are the same as the original's
lines (they are worked out from the same tables), so none of the wall pieces overlap.

* face_00 to face_31: the wall seen from each place, as the stone face of a block that is solid
  (and also the face of a door, which gets its door from the next set).
* face_33 to face_63: the door set in the wall of the place that is 32 less. Drawn over the wall.
* face_32: the opening of the door that the party is standing in. It is only used to rub out the
  part of face_00 that is in front of the party, so any opaque pixels will do.
* ladder_00 to ladder_20: a ladder up, in the ceiling of that place. A ladder down is the same
  picture flipped upside down, so draw the up one.
* chest_00 to chest_20: a chest in that place.

Ladders and chests are drawn over the walls, farthest first.

Regenerating the stand-ins
--------------------------
python gen_ultima3_dungeons.py ../../../engines/ultima/ultima3/gfx/dungeon_view.cpp ../files/ultima3/u3vga.pal ../files/ultima3/dungeons

Adding a dungeon .ULT file and a preview .png name after those arguments also draws a few views
made of the pictures, to look at.
